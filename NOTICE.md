# Notice on ASD-STE100 and this repository

## What this repository is not

This repository does **not** contain ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, in
whole or in part. It contains no part of the STE dictionary, no verbatim rule
text, and no examples copied from the standard.

## Why

ASD-STE100 is published by the Aerospace, Security and Defence Industries
Association of Europe (ASD) and is free to obtain. It is **not** free to
redistribute. Issue 9 states that no reproduction or publication of the
document, in whole or in part, shall be made without the written authority of
an officer of ASD. It grants irrevocable free reproduction rights to eight
listed categories of organization: ASD member associations and their member
companies, AIA and AIAC members, other ICCAIA members, customers of those
companies, defence ministries of ASD/AIA/AIAC member countries, Airlines for
America, airworthiness authorities, and universities and research institutes
for educational purposes.

This project belongs to none of those categories. So the standard stays out of
this repository.

## What this repository does contain

- Rule **numbers** and **paraphrases** written from scratch. A rule number is a
  citation, not a reproduction.
- Examples written for this project. Where an example illustrates a rule that
  the standard also illustrates, the sentence is ours, not theirs.
- Software that checks the structural rules, written from the rule descriptions.

## What this means for you

The `ste100` skill in this repository is **STE-informed**. It is not an
STE-compliance tool and cannot certify a document as STE-compliant. Full
compliance requires the approved dictionary of Part 2, which is where the word
choice rules (1.1 thru 1.4, 1.6, and 9.2) live. Those rules cannot be checked
without it.

If you need real compliance, get the standard. It is free. Request it at
<https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html>. That page is a request form
that emails you a link, not a direct download.
