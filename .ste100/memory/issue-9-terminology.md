- Section 2 is **Multi-word nouns** (rules 2.1, 2.2). Not "noun clusters".
- The term is **technical noun** (rule 1.1 onward). Not "technical name".
- The dictionary holds **875 approved** words and **1274 not approved**. Not
  "about 900" and "about 1,200".

**Why:** Issue 9 (January 2025) made these changes to align the standard's
terminology with ISO 1087 and ISO 704. Secondary sources and the existing
third-party STE skill still use the old terms, so text that copies them is
visibly out of date to anyone at the STEMG.

**How to apply:** use the Issue 9 words in every document and every rewrite.
